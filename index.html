Why this day matters

Breast cancer is treatable when it is found early. Many women in The Gambia still go without screening because of cost, distance or fear. This event brings screening and information to you, at no charge.

BCW is a national nonprofit that provides free screening, awareness campaigns and patient support, working under the leadership of the National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP), Ministry of Health. Global Tower joins us as event partner to make this day possible.
